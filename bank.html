3 What I Need From 兆豐 — 我需要的服務

English

I chose 兆豐銀行 because it is a government-owned bank (公股銀行) and handles the programs I need under one roof:

1 Personal account — to start the relationship today

2 Preparatory account (籌備戶) — to deposit company capital before registration

3 Formal business account — after company registration, to receive revenue and grant funds

4 Youth Entrepreneurship Loan (青年創業貸款) — NT$1.5M working capital. The government guarantees 80-95% of this loan.

5 雄挺利 — Loan Interest Subsidy — covers interest payments for up to 5 years

That is why I am here.

5 How the Loan Fits Into My Plan — 貸款用途說明

English

The Youth Loan is the engine of everything else.

I need the NT$1.5M working capital to:

1. Fund my company's daily operations and development (building the AI product)

2. Cover the 50% match requirement for MOEA SBIR Phase 1 (I spend NT$1.5M, gov reimburses NT$750K)

3. Purchase development equipment (Mac Studio for AI/ML development — ~NT$292K)

4. Pay for virtual business address, bookkeeping, and other monthly overhead

The 雄挺利 interest subsidy makes the loan effectively interest-free for 5 years.
